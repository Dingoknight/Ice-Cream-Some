Gladiators delight started as a humble ice cream stand on the side of State bridge in 1848. Despite not having a nickel to our name, we had a vision: ice cream that we could feel happy giving to the next generation.
Despite a rough start, things really picked up during the Roaring 20's, when our 5 dollar foot ice cream sandwiches sold faster than the Iphone 16.
Even though we have had our share of difficulties, we have maintained our vision, and are now happy to announce that the very intelligent students Levi Hutchens and Hudson Albright have joined Johns creek and with Levi's incredible powers of persuasion, he has convined the school board to merge Snoopy's Delight with Johns Creek High School.
