About Us


Gladiators delight started as a humble ice cream stand on the side of State bridge in 1848. Despite not having 20 dollars to our name, we had a vision: ice cream that we could feel happy giving to the next generation. Despite a rough start, things really picked up during the great depression, when our 5 dollar foot ice cream sandwiches sold faster than the Iphone-16. Despite half of our staff quitting due excessive High School brainrot in 2023, we have maintained our vision, and are now happy to announce that the very intelligent children Levi Hutchens and Hudson Alvright have joined Johns creek and with Levi's incredible powers of persuasion, he has convined the school board to merge Gladiators Delight with Johns Creek High School. Now he and his fellow genius Hudson are amazing their peers with their amazing programming skills.
